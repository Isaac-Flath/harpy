# Content style

- Use actual presenter photographs. Composite original pixels with crop/resize/placement; never regenerate, retouch identity, or invent expressions. Preserve natural color and photographic quality.
- Use real slide/document/screenshot pixels, keeping values, relationships, colors, and privacy blurs. Do not replace available evidence with decorative generated diagrams.
- Keep compositions simple and readable at phone/thumbnail size. Avoid decorative branding, glows, gradients, redundant labels, and generic marketing text. Preserve editable sources and asset provenance.
- For YouTube thumbnails: short, specific hook supported by the video; bold black text in white rectangular boxes with thick black borders, square corners, and generous padding. This is title-box styling, not a recoloring of the photo or evidence. The older rust-panel thumbnail treatment is retired.
- Thumbnail export: normally 1280×720 JPEG. Check full size and around 320×180 for spelling, margins, readability, real-photo fidelity, and evidence accuracy. Verify the intended video if an upload is requested.
- Use Figma MCP for Figma work and Premiere for video editing. Follow the current project's approved design; there is no inherited Camtasia template, font recipe, or global webinar palette.
