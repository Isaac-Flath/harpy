# Isaac's visual preferences

- Use real presenter photographs: crop, resize, and place original pixels. Don't regenerate faces, retouch identity, invent expressions, or recolor the photo.
- Use actual slide/document/screenshot pixels, preserving values, relationships, colors, and privacy blurs. Don't substitute decorative generated diagrams for available evidence.
- Simple compositions; no decorative branding, glows, gradients, redundant labels, or generic marketing text.
- YouTube thumbnails: a short, specific hook in bold black text inside white rectangular boxes, with thick black borders, square corners, and generous padding. This treatment applies to title boxes, not the photo or evidence.
